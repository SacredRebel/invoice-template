# SESSION PLAN — the visit with Karol

**For Sacred Rebel only. Not for her.**
She asked for help with *one invoice for her insurance company* and offered lunch.
Everything below is built. Don't lead with the system — lead with her invoice.

---

## Before you go — 15 minutes at home

- [ ] Fork or template this repo into **her** GitHub *(or create her account on the spot — see SETUP.md §1–2)*
- [ ] Fill in `data/business.json` with anything you already know about her
- [ ] Deploy it once to **your** Vercel so you've seen it work end to end
- [ ] Bring: laptop, charger, phone hotspot in case SANE's wifi is bad

**Have SETUP.md open in a tab.** Follow it, don't improvise.

---

## The order that matters

### 1 · Solve today's problem first *(15 min)*

**Make the insurance invoice with her. That's what she asked for.**

Get from her, out loud, while you type:
- Legal/business name, address, phone, email
- Any licence number she puts on paperwork
- The insurance company's name and address
- **Claim or policy number** → goes in the *Reference* field
- The line items and what she charges
- How she wants to be paid

Fill it in, press **Save and open**, press **Print → Save as PDF**. Done.
**She now has the thing she texted you for.** Everything after this is a bonus.

### 2 · Let her notice *(2 min)*

Don't pitch. Just say:

> *"That's saved. Next one takes about a minute — it remembers everything."*

Then wait. **She'll ask.** She already told you she writes invoices constantly and
that yours look professional. Let the question be hers.

### 3 · Then hand it over *(20 min)*

Now do SETUP.md properly with her watching:
- Her GitHub account and the token
- Her Vercel deploy
- Bookmark the URL on her work computer
- Delete the sample `INV-0001`

### 4 · The part that makes it hers *(10 min)*

Connect Claude to her repo and have **her** type the first change:

> *"Change my hourly rate to $XX"*

Then something visual:

> *"Make the invoice header dark green"*

**Let her press enter.** Watching her own words change her own tool is the whole
thing — that's what she'll describe to other people at SANE.

---

## Money

**Charge nothing.** She offered lunch; take the lunch.

She is a manager at an event centre who meets people constantly and already
recommends you. The referral is worth more than the fee.

**Say this before you leave:**

> *"Normally I'd set this up for around $150–200. This one's on me. If it saves
> you time, tell people I do this."*

That gives her **a number to quote** when she recommends you — which is the whole
point. Without the number, the referral is vague. With it, it's a lead.

---

## What to do if she asks for more

**"Can it email the invoice?"** — Not yet. Needs an email service and a key.
Say it's a bigger job and you'll price it separately.

**"Can my colleague use it?"** — Yes, same setup, separate repo. **That one's paid.**

**"Can it track hours too?"** — Yes. You already built that (`Work-logger-`).
Don't build it today. Note it and quote it.

## The strategic point

This is the first client in the AI-services direction, and it arrived **inbound**.
She didn't respond to a pitch — she watched you dictate into your phone on her job
site and produce a finished invoice. **The demo was the work itself.**

That is the sample-first method proving itself without you planning it. Whatever
you learn in this session applies directly to the cold outreach:
**show the finished thing, make the answer yes or no.**
